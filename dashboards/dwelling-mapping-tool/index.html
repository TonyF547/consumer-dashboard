Dwelling Mapping Tool

Select a TLA or region, narrow to specific towns if you like, set the maximum land size to exclude rural properties, then draw up to four cohorts on the map to build targeted address lists. Save a selection to come back and refine it later.

1. Choose an area
Load an area first to see its towns.
2. Draw up to 4 cohorts
Unassigned dwelling Cohort 1 Cohort 2 Cohort 3 Cohort 4
Use the polygon tool on the map (top-right) to draw your first cohort - e.g. a town, suburb or a few streets.
3. Save or reload this selection
Saving downloads a small file to your Downloads folder with the area, land-size setting and cohort shapes - reload it later to refine the polygons or land size and export a fresh CSV.
Land area and dwelling detection come from LINZ NZ Primary Parcels and NZ Building Outlines, joined to LINZ NZ Addresses. TLACode, postcode, zoning and campaign fields are left blank in the CSV export pending your zone lookup and downstream system - everything derivable from the address/parcel/building data is filled in.
